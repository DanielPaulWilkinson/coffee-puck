# User guide

## Install it
- **Android or Chrome:** open the app, then tap **Install app** in the browser menu.
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Once installed, it opens full screen and works offline. Press and hold the icon for the **New brew** and **Bean shelf** shortcuts.

## Add a bean
On the **Beans** tab, tap **+ Add bean**. Enter the name, roaster and roast date, then tap **Save and brew** to go straight to your first brew.

## Log a brew (about 20 seconds)
1. Tap **+ New brew**. The bean and your last recipe for it are already filled in.
2. Change only what you changed. That's usually the grind or the time.
3. Tap a taste score from 1 to 5, and any notes that fit: sour, bitter, balanced or sweet.
4. Tap **Save and get a hint**.

"Yield" means the drink out for espresso and the water in for filter methods.

## Dial-in hints explained
Each hint gives one change to make, never a list. It reads your taste first, then your time.

| You noted | Your time | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Sour | Fast | Grind finer |
| Sour | Any | Extract more: finer, a longer pull, or hotter water |
| Bitter | Slow | Grind coarser |
| Bitter | Any | Extract less: coarser, stop earlier, or water 2°C cooler |
| Sour and bitter | Any | Check your puck prep (uneven extraction) |
| Nothing, score 4–5 | Any | Keep this recipe |
| Nothing | Fast or slow | Adjust the grind towards the target time |

The bar under the hint shows your time against the target band for that method. The ★ **Best recipe** badge marks your highest-scoring brew for each bean; if two tie, the newer one wins.

## Edit or delete
Open a brew and tap **Edit or delete this brew**. For a bean, open it and tap **Edit or delete this bean**. Deleting a bean also deletes its brews.

## Backups
Your journal lives only on this device, so clearing your browser data erases it.
- **History → Export backup** downloads a `.json` file.
- **Import backup** has two options:
  - **Merge** adds anything new. Items that are in both get the backup's version.
  - **Replace** swaps your whole journal for the backup, after asking you to confirm.

To move to a new phone, export on the old one and import on the new one.
