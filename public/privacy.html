Privacy policy

Brew Journal is a personal coffee journal made by Daniel Wilkinson. In short: your data stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no ads and no third-party scripts. The app makes no network requests with your data.

Where your data lives

The beans and brews you log are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use. Nobody else, including the developer, can see them.

Backups

You can export a JSON backup file from the History screen. That file is yours; where you keep it is up to you. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes your journal unless you have a backup.

Hosting

The app is served as static files by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.
